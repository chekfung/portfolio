I am pleased to announce that we will be presenting our work, LASANA: Large-Scale Surrogate Modeling for Analog Neuromorphic Architecture Exploration, at MLCAD 2025 in Santa Cruz, CA!

LASANA leverages machine learning to create fast and accurate surrogate models of analog sub-blocks in hybrid mixed-signal neuromorphic architectures, achieving up to three orders of magnitude speedup over SPICE simulations while maintaining high accuracy.

The project is open-sourced and available below:
