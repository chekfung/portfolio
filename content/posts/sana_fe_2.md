---
title: SANA-FE 2 Accepted to IEEE Computer!
date: 2026-07-27
---

I am pleased to announce that our paper, Bridging the Gap in Neuromorphic Co-Design with the SANA-FE Co-Simulation Framework, was accepted into IEEE Computer for a special issue magazine on emerging neuromorphic system design! I am a 2nd author on this paper, but this work integrates LASANA, and some of my more recent work on surrogate-aware training. It also proposes a few hybrid analog-digital large-scale neuromorphic architectures combining SANA-FE's fast digital backend with analog plug-ins from LASANA.
